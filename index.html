Quality
Field of view 60°

Left stick: forward / back and slide sideways. Let go to hover.
Right stick: pull back = nose up, push = nose down; left / right = banked turn.
Drag elsewhere: look around; the view stays where you leave it. Double-tap to look ahead again.
🗺 Map: leave flight mode to find a new spot with the usual map gestures, then tap ✈ Fly.
Speed grows with height. Keyboard: WASD + arrow keys.
Tip: in Safari use Share → Add to Home Screen to fly full-screen.
